- Today hides an empty Mac token panel until a source is connected and gives the companion more space. The basic timer is an explicit free action. The unconfigured seasonal shop is hidden unless its product loads or it is already owned.
- Public support and privacy pages are live, return HTTP 200 and describe the actual local data, optional permissions and Apple purchases. No analytics or account was added.
